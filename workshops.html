Workshops

As with any rewarding discipline worth committing to, when it comes to writing, great habits matter more than great talents. Our workshops are designed to help writers shed habits that hold them back, and build or refine the practices that sustain quality work over time.

Common Questions

Do I need to be an experienced writer?

No. Our workshops serve all levels, from beginners to professionals. The focus is on practice and process, not prior experience.

What should I bring?

Notebooks, pens, and an open mind. We provide additional materials. You will NOT need your phone.

What is the refund policy?

Full refund up to 10 days before the workshop. 50% refund 3-10 days before. No refunds within 3 days of start date.

Can I attend virtually?

Not currently. The methodology requires in-person, phone-free practice. Virtual options may be available in 2027.

How are cohorts formed?

We use archetype assessments and optional writing samples to match participants with complementary styles and goals.

Are scholarships available?

Yes. We offer quarterly scholarships funded by participant donations. Apply or nominate someone by email.
